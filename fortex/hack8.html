Supporting Street Animals

The Role of NGOs

Non-governmental organizations (NGOs) dedicated to street dogs and cats play a crucial role in improving the lives of homeless animals by providing essential care in environments often fraught with danger, hunger, and abuse.

These organizations work tirelessly to manage stray populations through humane sterilization (ABC-ARV) programs and vaccination drives, which reduce rabies cases and control overpopulation.

Our Partners & Rescues

Beyond population control, these NGOs run shelters, ambulance services, and 24/7 clinics to treat injured, sick, or abandoned animals, offering them a second chance at life.
